About Us

Komnotra is a free, independently developed workspace that brings projects, meetings, tasks, weekly plans, notes and documents together in one place. It is designed for busy professionals who want to organize their work and keep their files in their own Google Drive.

What you can do

  • Organize projects and folders, and attach files and links
  • Schedule meetings (single day or multi-day), keep agendas and minutes, and export to Word, PDF or Excel
  • Plan your week and track tasks with reminders
  • Share selected content with people you invite

Our approach

We do not keep a separate database of your personal files. Your content stays on your device and in your own Google Drive. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
