Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Komnotra (“we”, “our”, “the Service”), available at https://komnotra.net/, is a free workspace for managing projects, meetings, tasks, weekly plans, notes and documents. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Service uses and how it is protected.

1. Information we use

  • Content you create. Projects, folders, meetings, tasks, notes, plans and files you add are stored in your own browser (local storage on your device) and, if you connect Google Drive, in your own Google Drive.
  • Google account information. If you choose to connect a Google account, the Service receives your basic profile (name, email address) so it can show who is signed in and manage sharing between users you invite.
  • Google Drive access. With your permission, the Service can create, open and preview files in your Google Drive so that your documents, backups and shared content are kept in your own account.

2. We do not keep your files

We do not maintain a separate server database of your personal files or documents. Files are transferred directly between your device and your own Google Drive account.

3. How we use information

Information is used only to provide the features you request: storing and displaying your content, syncing with your Google Drive, sharing selected items with people you invite, and sending in-app reminders. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Komnotra’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Google is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above and is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

5. Sharing

Content is shared only when you explicitly choose items and invite a specific Google account. You can change or remove access at any time. We may disclose information only if required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Your content stays on your device and in your Google Drive until you delete it. You can disconnect Komnotra at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and clear local data using your browser settings. To ask about removing any information, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

Access to Google services uses Google’s secure authorization (OAuth 2.0) and encrypted HTTPS connections. Please protect your Google account and keep backups of important documents. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Service is intended for general office and productivity use and is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version.
